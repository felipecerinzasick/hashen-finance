The gap

Bitcoin exposure is no longer one line on one statement.

Private owners often hold Bitcoin through several wrappers, venues, and counterparties. The reporting and governance record usually does not keep up.

Exposure is scattered

Spot Bitcoin, ETFs, treasury equities, cash, and collateral do not arrive as one clean Bitcoin exposure number from a private bank statement.

Custody needs explaining

Advisers and boards need to understand wallets, access rights, recovery paths, approval flows, and who can move what under which conditions.

The reasoning disappears

Reserve policy, concentration limits, treasury-stock exposure, and loan decisions are often made in meetings, messages, and memory.
