What it tracks

A calmer operating layer for private wealth.

Hashen brings together the pieces that usually live in brokerage screens, bank portals, spreadsheets, screenshots, and memory.

Net worth and month-end freezes

Track cash, listed securities, and Bitcoin across month-end snapshots so wealth progression is visible and auditable.

Bitcoin-first capital preservation

Keep Bitcoin exposure visible as a reserve asset, with live marks, target scenarios, and allocation context.

Investment pages and targets

Each stock can have its own page, quantity, buy time, thesis, and price target instead of being just a ticker row.

Private memos

Write down the reason before the market tests you. Store short-horizon bets, long-term theses, and exit rules.

Working app previews

Dashboards that feel like an owner console, not a trading terminal.

The private console is designed for repeated review: scan the month-end state, inspect concentration, open a holding, read the memo, and move on with a clearer head.
